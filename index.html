Van der Waals AI Labs | Beyond the Limits

VdW Labs builds dependable AI systems grounded in structured knowledge, verifiable evidence, and real-world constraints.

Specialized retrieval, semantic knowledge structures, and source-level verification turn fragmented information into intelligence people can inspect, use, and trust.

THE DILEMMA

Fluency is not enough

A fluent answer can still be unsupported, incomplete, or disconnected from the information that matters. Where decisions depend on complex institutional knowledge, confident generation without evidence is not a dependable foundation.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Knowledge with structure

We connect specialized retrieval with semantic graphs, preserving relationships and provenance across fragmented sources. Answers can be grounded in institutional knowledge and traced back to the material behind them.

Our Outlook

Looking Forward

We are building the foundations for AI systems that can work with complex institutional knowledge: specialized retrieval pipelines, semantic structures, and source-level verification.

Rather than make claims ahead of the engineering, we believe the working system should show what it can do—and where its limits are.

We prioritize grounded outputs, observable behavior, and clear provenance. The work continues after launch through monitoring, iteration, and maintenance.
